# Changelog

All notable changes to Vento are listed here, newest first. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and Vento uses
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

The What's new window reads this file. Every entry starts with the key of the
feature it belongs to, then a colon (`Wheel`, `Move`, `Pocket`, `Clipboard`,
`Copy from screen`, `Convert`, `PDF Pages`, `Archive`, `Info`, `Share`,
`Keep Awake`, `Updates`, `Settings`, `Welcome`); an entry with no known key
applies everywhere. An entry may end with where to find it, as a sentence
such as `Settings → About.` The Arabic text is in `CHANGELOG.ar.md`, with the
same versions and the same keys.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-04

### Summary
Vento keeps itself up to date, speaks seven more languages, and gains Rename, PDF pages, Share, Screenshots, Keep Awake and a new island.

### Added
- Updates: Vento looks for a new version once a day and installs it with one click from the menu bar panel. Settings → About.
- Updates: After an update, a What's new window shows what changed, feature by feature.
- Pocket: On screens without a notch, the virtual island rests as a small glass pill on the top edge and opens when you push the pointer against the edge. Choose the Island or the Circle, its place, how see-through it rests and a shimmer. Settings → Island.
- Pocket: The island shows what a job is doing, with Cancel, then the result with one button such as Show or Undo. On a Mac with a notch, the notch island does the same.
- Pocket: The island has a tab each for the Pocket, the clipboard history and the screenshots.
- Wheel: The Rename action renames many files and folders at once with replace, add, number, case, extension and template rules, with every new name shown beside the old one before anything changes. Settings → Wheel.
- PDF Pages: Release one PDF or several to see every page: merge files, reorder, rotate or delete pages, then save a new PDF, extract or split pages, or turn them into images. Settings → Wheel.
- Share: Send what you drag with AirDrop, Mail, Messages and more, straight from the wheel. Settings → Wheel.
- Pocket: The island lists the last week of screenshots, and a card for each new one lets you drag it, copy it or its text, send it to the Pocket or move it to the Trash. Settings → Screenshots.
- Keep Awake: Keep the Mac awake for a few minutes, a few hours, until a time or until you stop it, from the menu bar panel; pick the times it offers and its default. Settings → Keep Awake.
- Pocket: The Pocket opens by kind, with images, documents, folders and more in rows, or as a list with filters and previews. Settings → Pocket.
- Settings: Arabic, laid out right to left, and Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil) and Turkish. Settings → General.
- Settings: One Settings window with a page for each feature and a How to card on each page, and seven themes for it and the menu bar panel. Settings → General.
- Welcome: A short setup on the first launch: pick a language and a look, try the wheel on a sample file, and choose what it shows.

### Fixed
- Settings: Choosing a folder or a PDF while a drag was going on could close Vento.
